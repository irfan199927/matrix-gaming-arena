Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome. On busy evenings and weekends, message us on WhatsApp first so your station is ready when you arrive.

Is the price per person?

No. You pay for the station and the number of controllers. Four friends on one PS5 for an hour is ₹300 in total.

When are happy hours?

Weekday mornings before noon, with up to 35% off session rates.

Can I race in VR?

Yes. Pick "Sim + VR" on the racing simulator: ₹240 for 30 minutes or ₹400 for an hour.

How do passes work?

You buy a block of hours up front at a lower rate and use them across visits until the pass expires. Message us to buy one.
